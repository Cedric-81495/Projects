## The consent decision, and why it matters

Jake, 2026-08-19:

> the phone number can be required, but the SMS consent checkbox should be
> optional and unchecked by default

The approved wording says so itself — **"Consent is not a condition of
purchase."** A required checkbox would contradict the sentence sitting beside
it, and would lose signups from people who want the Blueprint but not the
texts.

So a declined lead is still captured, stored, and forwarded to GHL carrying
`sms_consent: false`. Jake's workflow branches on that value to decide who
receives SMS.

**What did not change:** `consent_text` is still required and still stores the
exact sentence shown. A declined lead is evidence too — it records that the
person was given the option and chose not to take it.

Verified behaviour:

```
ticked      → ACCEPTED, consent_given = true
not ticked  → ACCEPTED, consent_given = false
missing     → REJECTED
```

## The approved wording

Do not reword any part of this without going back to Jake. The STOP and HELP
keywords and the "not a condition of purchase" line are carrier requirements,
not copy.

> I consent to receive marketing text messages from GWOP University at the
> phone number provided. Message frequency may vary. Message and data rates may
> apply. Reply STOP to opt out and HELP for assistance. Consent is not a
> condition of purchase.

Privacy Policy, Terms & Conditions and SMS Terms now sit directly beneath it —
the terms being agreed to have to be reachable from the same place the
agreement is given. They open in a new tab so a tap never loses a half-filled
form at the booth.
