## The flow now

```
QR → /830 → interest (Q1) → contact form → Q2…Q7 → Blueprint
                              ↓ lead saved here
```

Capture sits before the six remaining questions, so somebody who abandons at Q4
is a lead with four answers attached rather than a lost scan. That was the point
of Felicia's reordering and it should survive any later redesign.

`/thanks` still exists and is still where someone lands if the assessment cannot
start — the lead is saved either way, and confirmation beats a broken flow.

## Decisions worth knowing about

**Answers post one at a time, not once at the end.** A phone locking at Q5 costs
nothing. A single submission at the end would mean a dropped connection halfway
loses everything they gave.

**A failed save never blocks the attendee.** The answer is held and replayed with
the next tap. Their time at the table is worth more than a perfectly complete
row, and there is nothing useful they could do about a sync error anyway.

**Skip is always visible.** An honest skip is better data than a guessed answer,
and blocking someone on a question they do not want to answer is how you lose the
remaining ones.

**Tapping an option advances.** No Next button, which halves the taps across the
flow.

**No navigation between questions.** Pure state on one page load. A client-side
route change on `/830` is what let the chat widget inject a second Turnstile and
422 every submission — see `NoThirdPartyWidgets.tsx`.

**Stage collapses four answers to two.** Starting Over and Getting Stable need
the same first move; Building and Ready to Grow both have something to work with.
Four groups would double the copy Surpaul has to approve for a distinction the
roadmaps would not make.

**Q1 stays on `leads`.** It is the interest question and it already has the
verbatim-tag contract with Jake. Duplicating it into `assessments` would create
two sources of truth for one answer.
